HealthApp-01

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Health Intelligence is designed to process the Apple Health information you choose to make available on your device. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or send it to third parties.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Health Intelligence iOS app (the “App”) handles information. It describes the current TestFlight V1 implementation only. “HealthApp-01,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the App’s developer.

2. Apple Health data

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the App requests read-only access to the health categories it needs to provide its features. Depending on what you authorize and what is recorded in Apple Health, this may include sensible categories such as heart and breathing signals, sleep information, activity and exercise information, walking or mobility measurements, workout records, mindfulness sessions, and time spent in daylight.

The App does not write, edit, or delete any Apple Health data. It does not request or read Cycle Tracking data; Cycle is not currently enabled with HealthKit in the App. You can review or change Apple Health permissions at any time in the Health app.

3. How the App uses health data

The App uses authorized Apple Health data on your device to show health information and to generate app features such as summaries, trends, and derived scores or insights. These outputs may compare recent readings with recent patterns and may be unavailable when there is not enough relevant data. They are consumer-wellness features, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

4. Local storage

The App stores its settings locally on your device, including items such as your display name, onboarding status, unit and appearance preferences, and selected goals. It may also keep limited locally stored derived information needed for its features, such as derived baselines, feature summaries, and HealthKit query anchors. Apple Health remains the source for the underlying health records.

5. Sharing, sale, advertising, and tracking

The App does not transmit your Apple Health data to our servers or to third parties. We do not sell Apple Health data, use it for advertising or marketing, or use it for cross-app or cross-site tracking. The App does not include analytics, tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or cookies.

6. Your choices

You may decline Apple Health access, revoke access, or change which categories are available to the App through the Health app. The App remains usable when health data is unavailable, although some features may show an unavailable or no-data state. You can remove locally stored App information by deleting the App from your device; Apple Health records are managed separately in the Health app.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App’s data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact
