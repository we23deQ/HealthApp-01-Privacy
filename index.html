InnerTempo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

InnerTempo is designed to process the Apple Health information you choose to make available on your device. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or send it to third parties.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how InnerTempo for iPhone, its companion Apple Watch app, and its Watch widgets handle information. “HealthApp-01,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the App’s developer.

2. Apple Health data

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the App requests read-only access to the health categories it needs to provide its features. Depending on what you authorize and what is recorded in Apple Health, this may include heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep stages, activity and exercise, walking and mobility measurements, cardio fitness, workout records, mindfulness sessions, time in daylight, and recorded menstrual flow.

The App does not write, edit, or delete any Apple Health data. Menstrual flow records are read only when available and authorized; the App does not infer missing records. You can review or change Apple Health permissions at any time in the Health app.

3. How the App uses health data

The App uses authorized Apple Health data on your device to show health information and to generate app features such as summaries, trends, and derived scores or insights. These outputs may compare recent readings with recent patterns and may be unavailable when there is not enough relevant data. They are consumer-wellness features, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

4. Local storage

The App stores its settings locally on your device, including items such as your display name, onboarding status, unit and appearance preferences, and selected goals. It may also keep limited locally stored derived information needed for its features, such as derived baselines, feature summaries, and HealthKit query anchors. Apple Health remains the source for the underlying health records. Water and mood entries you choose to record are saved locally. The iPhone and paired Apple Watch exchange supported summaries and entries through Apple WatchConnectivity. Watch widgets read a local shared cache on the Watch; they do not contact a developer server.

5. Purchases and support

InnerTempo is free to download and offers an optional, one-time, non-consumable Lifetime purchase for History, Trends, and Deeper Insight. Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store and StoreKit. The App checks Apple-verified transactions to determine access; we do not receive your payment-card details. No InnerTempo account is required.

If you contact support by email, the information you choose to send is handled by the email providers involved and used to respond to your request. Please do not include health records or other sensitive information unless necessary.

6. Sharing, sale, advertising, and tracking

The App does not transmit your Apple Health data to our servers or to third parties. We do not sell Apple Health data, use it for advertising or marketing, or use it for cross-app or cross-site tracking. The App does not include analytics, tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or cookies.

7. Your choices

You may decline Apple Health access, revoke access, or change which categories are available to the App through the Health app. The App remains usable when health data is unavailable, although some features may show an unavailable or no-data state. The Privacy & Data screen provides a Clear App Data action for supported local iPhone entries and preferences. You can also delete the app from each device. Apple Health records and App Store purchase history are managed separately by Apple; clearing app data does not delete them. Local Watch caches and pending entries are stored separately on the Watch.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App’s data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact and support

For privacy questions or support, contact us at andrealoc2026@gmail.com.

For setup: open InnerTempo on iPhone and choose whether to connect Apple Health. Pair the companion Watch with your iPhone for shared summaries. Missing information remains unavailable when Apple Health has no readable records. To restore Lifetime access, use Restore Purchases in Me or on the Lifetime screen while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase.
